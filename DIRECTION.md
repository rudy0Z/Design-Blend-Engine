# Design Blend — Visual, Motion & Product Direction (v3)

## 1. The Brief & What We Refuse
- **Audience:** Architects, industrial designers, photographers, fashion/art directors, typographers, brand designers, motion artists, 3D creators, and product/UI designers.
- **The One Job:** Feel your own visual taste come alive in 2 minutes, save your permanent Taste Profile, and send a Blend link to another creative to get a multi-card Spotify-Blend-style comparison story.
- **Explicitly Refused Slop Faces (`slop.md`):**
  1. *Refused — "The Paper Edition":* No warm beige newsprint + Instrument Serif + italic vermilion word + uppercase mono section eyebrows (`01 — STUDIO`, `SPECIMEN ENGINE`, `EPISTEMIC COORDINATES`) + rigid sharp-cornered developer boxes.
  2. *Refused — Academic Jargon:* Ban words like *epistemic, dyad, dossier, Bradley–Terry, heuristic, telemetry, specimen index*. Speak like a sharp, warm creative friend at a studio table.
  3. *Refused — Mid-Session Spoilers:* Never show a "tentative archetype" while the user is reacting. Build anticipation and earn the reveal ceremony at the end.

## 2. Physical World & Concept
**"Design Blend is a backlit studio light-table, a tactile FM tuner dial, a splayed hand of collector slides, and a perforated Blend pass."**
- Inspired by physical objects creatives love: Leica slide mounts, teenage engineering / Opal rotary detents, Polaroid stacks, vinyl gatefolds, and Spotify Blend's multi-card story ceremony.

## 3. Tokens & Cast
- **Grounds:**
  - *Dark Studio (Default — makes photography, architecture, fashion, and UI glow like backlit film slides):* Deep Smoked Onyx (`#0A0B0E`), Elevated Slate Velvet (`#13151B`), Tactile Rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`), Specular Top Highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`).
  - *Daylight Ceramic (Light Toggle):* Cool Alabaster Stone (`#F2F3F7`), Ceramic Surface (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Rim (`rgba(15, 18, 26, 0.09)`).
- **Accent & Generative Taste Auras:**
  - Primary Interactive Signal: Electric Tangerine-Coral (`#FF5A36`) — warm, physical, high-craft hardware indicator.
  - Every designer's profile generates a unique **Taste Aura** (two harmonic pigments derived deterministically from their Warm↔Cold, Minimal↔Maximal, and Order↔Chaos coordinates) so when two people Blend, their two auras physically merge on screen!
- **Typography (`type.md`):**
  - *Display & Voice:* **Bricolage Grotesque** (`opsz` variable, sculptural ink-traps, expressive confidence without stiff academic serif clichés).
  - *Interface & Reading:* **Schibsted Grotesk** (crisp, fluid Scandinavian legibility).
  - *Dial Numerals & Pass Serials Only:* **Martian Mono** (`tabular-nums`).

## 4. Living Motion Spells (`motion-craft.md`, `scrolltide`, `designspells`)
1. **Splayed Fan Deck Hero:** A hinged hand of physical collector cards across all 9 creative worlds that splays open with spring physics and lifts individual cards with 3D pointer-tracked specular sheen.
2. **Physical Throw Stack + Optical Magnifier Loupe:** Drag/flick slides with real spring velocity, tilt, and edge-glow feedback; hold/click the Loupe icon for an optical glass zoom lens.
3. **Skeuomorphic FM Tick-Tape Tuner:** Fling or scrub a physical rotary tick ruler with rolling digits while watching architectural/typographic/interface space morph live.
4. **Perforated Tear-Off Reveal Ceremony:** Pull/tap across the perforated ticket line at the end of capture to tear the stub free and reveal your newly minted Taste Card.
5. **Multi-Card Blend Story Deck:** 5 swipeable, distinct Spotify-Blend-style cards per pair (Dual Aura Score, Shared Obsessions, Friendly Fight, World-by-World Radar/Bars, Steal This Reference).
